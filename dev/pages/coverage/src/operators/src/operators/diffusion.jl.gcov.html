     482              : in **compact flux form**: fluxes `q_{i+½} = κ_{i+½}(u_{i+1} − u_i)/Δ` live on faces, with
     483              : `κ` averaged to faces by `averaging`, and the cell balance is `(q_{i+½} − q_{i−½})/Δ`.

     485              : Prefer this to the algebraic composition `divergence(g) * scaling(κ) * gradient(g)`.      490              : conditions (the composed form is not), and it has an [`operator_diagonal`](@ref) (the
     491              : composed form cannot), which is what makes it usable with the multigrid smoothers.
     492              : 
     493              : With `κ ≡ c` constant this reduces to `c * laplacian(g)` up to floating-point operation
