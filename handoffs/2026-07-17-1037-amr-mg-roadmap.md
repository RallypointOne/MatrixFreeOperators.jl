# Handoff: AMR/MG roadmap — sequencing decision of 2026-07-17

## Goal / why this matters

Record the agreed plan for finishing the AMR + multigrid work so any future session can
pick up the next phase without relitigating the ordering. This is a decision record; the
per-phase technical briefs live in the sibling handoffs referenced below.

## Background & current state

- The `amr-mg` integration branch (phase 0–1 AMR: `Forest` topology, `BlockForest`,
  `BlockField`, halo exchange, forest operator algebra from PR #3; the PR #8 allocation
  fix; CI pinning) is being merged to `main` as of this writing — main is the trunk from
  here on, and future phases branch off main.
- Everything merged is correct and gated: missing capabilities throw
  (`_require_uniform` at `src/BlockForest.jl:70`, `Composed`-on-forest at
  `src/operators/forest.jl:22`) rather than produce wrong results.
- Issue #5 (prepare-time leaf caching) is delivered by PR #8 and being closed; the
  residual ~384 B/leaf is tracked in issue #7.

## Decisions & conclusions

Sequencing (agreed with Kyle, 2026-07-17):

1. **Merge `amr-mg` → `main` now.** The remaining work is months of multi-PR phases;
   holding the integration branch open only accumulates drift. Pre-release, so main
   carries no stability promise.
2. **Next phase: coarse–fine interface interpolation**, folding in the
   [halo-exchange-schedule](2026-07-17-1009-halo-exchange-schedule.md) handoff (option 6)
   rather than doing it separately — the schedule is the natural home for interpolation
   descriptors; building them apart means building the exchange machinery twice. This
   phase also unlocks `Composed` on forests nearly for free (same inter-block exchange of
   intermediate fields). DESIGN.md §9 flags hanging-node/non-conforming stencils as the
   risk to prototype early on a small case.
3. **Then geometric multigrid**: restriction/prolongation as operators in the existing
   algebra, `coarsen(g)`, a smoother (Jacobi via `isdiagonal`, Chebyshev via repeated
   `apply!`), V-cycle. Wants the coarse–fine transfer machinery in place first
   (DESIGN.md §9 sketch).
4. **Deferred, not abandoned:**
   - [homogeneous-leaves-bc-face-pass](2026-07-17-1009-homogeneous-leaves-bc-face-pass.md)
     (option 5) — do when the BC-signature group-cache complexity actually bites.
   - [packed-forest-kernels](2026-07-17-1009-packed-forest-kernels.md) (option 7) — the
     GPU phase; **closes issue #7 as a side effect, so do not chase #7 separately.**
